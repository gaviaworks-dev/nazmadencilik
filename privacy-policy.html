Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains the principles governing the protection of the privacy of users visiting the website operated by Naz Mining Tourism Construction Import Export Industry & Trade Ltd. Co. (the "Company"). For detailed information on the processing of personal data, the KVKK Information Notice prevails.

1. Information Collected

If you merely visit our website, no personal data about you are collected by our Company. If you use the contact form, your name, surname, e-mail address, phone number, and the message subject and any information you share in its content are processed for the purpose of responding to your request.

2. Use of Information

Information submitted through the contact form is not used for any purpose other than evaluating and responding to your request and, upon request, establishing a commercial relationship; it is not added to advertising or marketing lists and is not shared with third parties for such purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The map display service on our website is provided by Google LLC; any data processing that takes place while the map area is displayed is subject to Google's own privacy policy. This section will be updated once the infrastructure provider for the technical transmission of the contact form has been selected.

4. Cookies

Our website does not set any cookies of its own and does not use any analytics or tracking technology. For details, please see the Cookie Policy.

5. Data Security

Our Company takes the necessary technical and administrative measures, within the means of available technology, to ensure an appropriate level of security in order to prevent the unlawful processing of and unlawful access to personal data, and to ensure their safekeeping. The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when necessary; the current version is always published on this page and indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top of the page.
