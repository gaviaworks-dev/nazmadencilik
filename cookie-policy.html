1. What Is a Cookie?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device through your browser when you visit a website. Cookies may be used for purposes such as making the site work, remembering preferences, or collecting usage statistics.

2. Use of Cookies on This Site

Our website does not set any cookies of its own. No cookies or similar technologies (pixels, fingerprinting, etc.) are used on the site for analytics, tracking, advertising or marketing purposes; only the infrastructure operation strictly necessary for the technical delivery of the site takes place. Your choice in the notice banner shown at the bottom of the page is stored only on your device, via local storage (localStorage), which is not a cookie.

3. Third-Party Content

The map in the Contact section is displayed through an embed (iframe) provided by Google LLC. When the map area loads, Google may set cookies as part of the operation of its own services; our Company has no control over these cookies. For detailed information, please review Google's privacy policy.

4. Managing Cookies

You can delete or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. If third-party cookies are blocked, third-party content such as the map display may not work.

5. Contact

For questions about the use of cookies, you can reach us at info@nazmadencilik.com.tr. For detailed information on the processing of personal data, please see the KVKK Information Notice.
