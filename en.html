01/Corporate

Corporate

Founded in Istanbul as Naz Mining Tourism Construction Import Export Industry & Trade Ltd. Co., our company has achieved success and excellence in all its areas of operation in a short span of time. The success of our company stems from the complete cooperation it maintains with the companies it works with in both export and import, and from being a well-known, respectable entity trusted for its reliability.

Our company has transcended Turkish borders to become a bridge between producers and consumers. Through a wide foreign-trade market network spanning three continents, we procure the products our clients request from a variety of companies — both manufacturers and direct sellers — on favourable terms, with reliable and honest service. As we do with our business partners and clients, we create an environment of honesty and trust with our employees, and we continuously improve our knowledge and technology to provide the highest-quality products and services.

Believing in the importance of investing in people and of customer relationship management in the modern world, our company has adopted as a basic principle — within a framework of morality and good faith — the philosophy of informed staff and informed customers, providing continuous training to its employees to raise their knowledge and culture and to increase the quality of its workforce. With its solid and dynamic structure, our company works to be a force that constantly accelerates our market in the areas in which it operates at home and abroad, and continues its research into the promising business lines of the age.
